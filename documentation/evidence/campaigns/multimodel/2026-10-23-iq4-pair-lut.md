## Scope

- IQ4_NL expert Down, K640/N2560; both FlashNext AD4.27 and UDQ2.
- STREAMED/PAGED, BM32/BM64; unchanged dot/scaling/reduction and activation format.
- Pair table adds 1024 bytes LDS and one initialization barrier per nonempty group.
  No persistent allocation, scratch increase, weight repack or cache shrink.
- Default only on measured Windows/RDNA3 family and this shape. Other devices,
  formats and shapes keep their original kernel. `kernels.vulkan.iq4_pair_lut=false`
  provides the same-binary fallback. No effect on the ordinary Decode GEMV route.

## Correctness and Modules

- Three independent CPU packing/oracle tests passed, including signed byte dots.
- GPU:192 size/tail/BM/address-mode cases; raw f32 bytes and canaries exact.
- Whole-layer:512 distinct experts,450MiB weights, exceeding L2;512/1024/2048/4096
  tokens x10 assignments, uniform/skewed packed buckets, BM32/64, STREAMED/PAGED.
  All32 cases exact before/after five ABBA rounds; signed QA and finite signed,
  zero, subnormal and large half scales covered. Every round warms >=300ms of GPU
  work. Full dispatch initialization is timed; upload/readback/encoding are separate.
- Whole-layer GPU latency reduction:30.695-52.640%; this is not model throughput.
